# Code of conduct

Acadia welcomes people with different research backgrounds, technical experience, and perspectives. Participation in this repository should be useful, respectful, and safe.

## How we work together

Discuss the work and its evidence. Explain disagreements without attacking the person. Give actionable feedback, credit other people's contributions, and accept corrections when facts change. Make room for new contributors and people who communicate differently.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, targeted personal attacks, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Do not use issues or pull requests for spam, intimidation, or repeated disruption after a maintainer asks you to stop.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces maintained for Acadia. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report content** option for abusive content. For a concern requiring confidential discussion with a repository maintainer, ask for a private reporting route without posting names, private messages, or sensitive details publicly. Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, request a change in behavior, or restrict participation. The response should reflect the behavior, its impact, and whether it continues. A participant may ask for the decision to be reconsidered and provide relevant context without repeating the harmful content publicly.

No one should be targeted for making a good-faith report or participating in its review. Keep reports focused on observable behavior, and respect the privacy of the people involved.
