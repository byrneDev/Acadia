# Research-to-delivery boards in Acadia v0.5

Use the Collector to map **Question → Sources → Reviewed Evidence → Findings → Gaps → Tasks → Deliverables**. The canvas stays freely arranged. A saved research record appears on it only after you choose to place it.

## Choose an item

Open **Add item** and search by item name or purpose.

| Group                   | Available items                                                                    |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Frame the investigation | Research brief/question, note, standalone question, hypothesis                     |
| Collect sources         | Existing source, website/link, document, image, audio, video, exact source passage |
| Examine evidence        | Finding/claim, accepted Reviewer Notes, assumption                                 |
| Apply methods           | Competing hypotheses, SWOT, root cause, risk assessment, technology readiness      |
| Investigate gaps        | Research gap, research task                                                        |
| Decide and deliver      | Decision, deliverable project plan, report                                         |

**Create new** opens the appropriate editor where a record needs more information. Save the record and select **Add to board**. **Use existing** places a saved record. A method can be created and placed from the picker; if placement fails, its saved worksheet remains recoverable. File choices use the existing import flow. A new delivery plan uses the analysis-to-plan workflow in Releaser.

Accepted Reviewer Notes require an explicitly accepted item review. Exact passages require a saved source version. Select an older version when the historical passage is the evidence you mean to show. Audio and video imports preserve the files; they do not add automatic transcription.

## Work with linked cards

Cards show their record type, current title and preview, and relevant status. Double-click a card or select its **Open** action to reach the source reader or authoritative editor. Saved edits update the card preview. Repeating **Add to board** focuses its existing placement.

Moving or removing a linked card changes only its board placement. The saved finding, task, report or other research record remains in its workspace. An unavailable reference stays visibly unavailable instead of turning into an ordinary note. Source passage and accepted-review cards retain historical passages and show when their source version is outdated.

Placing a finding, review, method, task, gap, decision or report does not create an independent source. Historical source copies from older method cards remain available to old citations but are excluded from new source retrieval.

## Connect missing evidence to work

In **Gaps & decisions**, create a gap with its missing information, importance, linked findings, resolution criteria and related research tasks. New gaps are **open**. Researchers may change them to **investigating** or **resolved**; a connection or AI suggestion cannot do so.

A decision records an action, rationale, alternatives, supporting findings, consequential assumptions and linked deliverable plans. It begins **proposed** and can be changed manually to **made** or **reconsider**. Saved changes create revisions. A related task or work package is still governed by its own completion criteria.

Use labeled connections such as **informs**, **identifies gap**, **addresses**, **depends on** and **produces**. Existing relationship labels remain available. A drawn connection expresses your mapping; it does not change evidential support, confidence, review status or completion.

## Portability and release privacy

Format **v4** preserves references, positions, relationships, gaps, decisions, reviews and report history. Import supports formats v1–v3. **V4 requires Acadia v0.5 or later**; v0.4 cannot open it. Database upgrades and replacement imports retain recovery backups.

Adding a report to the board does not release it. The separate display follows an explicitly released revision, keeping drafts and private workspace records in the Collector. PMIS and Power BI exports retain their existing local-file workflow.
